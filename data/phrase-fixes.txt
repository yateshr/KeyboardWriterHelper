# Real words used in the wrong place, as two-word phrases:
#     typed phrase=correct phrase
# The left side is the word before plus the word just typed; both are replaced.
#
# Only phrases that are practically never right as typed belong here: changing
# correct text is worse than missing a mistake. That is why these are absent:
#     could of      ("it could of course be")
#     to much       ("it did not amount to much")
#     better then   ("things were better then")
#     your welcome  ("your welcome email")
# Anyone who never writes those can add them to My fixes (Settings).
#
# A phrase listed as its own fix ("and fro") keeps fixes.txt away from it.
