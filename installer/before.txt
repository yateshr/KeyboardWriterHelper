Keyboard Writer Helper fixes spelling mistakes in any Windows program as you type. When you press Space after a misspelled word, it is replaced with the most likely intended word. Press Backspace right after a fix to undo it.


WHAT THIS SETUP DOES

  - Installs the app for your Windows account only. No administrator rights are needed.
  - Adds a Start menu entry. A desktop icon and "start with Windows" are your choice on a later page.
  - Keeps your settings, My words and My fixes in %APPDATA%\KeyboardWriterHelper, apart from the program. Updating or reinstalling never touches them.
  - Can look for a newer version about once a day. It only speaks up when there is one, and it asks before it installs anything.


WHAT IT NEVER DOES

  - It never sends what you type anywhere. Spelling, grammar and Smart Compose all run on this PC.
  - It installs no drivers, no services and no browser add-ons.
  - It does not change anything outside its own folder, your settings folder, one Start menu entry, and (only if you choose it) one startup entry.


BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

If Keyboard Writer Helper is running, close it first: right-click its icon in the system tray and choose Exit. Setup will tell you if it is still open.
